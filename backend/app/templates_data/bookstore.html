Our Story

Chapter & Verse was born from a simple belief: that a good bookshop is more than a place to buy books. It's a sanctuary for curious minds, a meeting place for dreamers, and a home for stories that change lives.

Founded in 1987, we've spent over three decades building not just a collection, but a community. Our booksellers don't just recommend titles — they match readers with the books they didn't know they needed.

From our hand-lettered shelf recommendations to our resident cat, Hemingway, every corner of our store is designed to make you linger a little longer.
