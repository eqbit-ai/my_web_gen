HOW WE WORK

A full-service studio

Everything from the first demo to the final master — and the freedom to use as much or as little of it as you need.

01

Pre-Production

A week on-site or remote, working with our in-house producers to shape arrangements, tempos, and keys. Then we plan the session.

02

Tracking

Two tracking rooms, one 28ft live room. API console, Studer A827 2-inch tape, and a mic cabinet with 120+ pieces.

03

Mixing

Hybrid mixing on the API plus hardware outboard. We print through the tape machine on request — it still makes a difference.

04

Mastering

A dedicated mastering room with Dangerous, Maselec, and Pultec EQs. CD, vinyl, and hi-res digital masters.

THE STUDIO

Twelve years. Thousand sessions.

Gravel Room was built in 2013 by producer Michael Obeng after years freelancing in London. The goal: a studio where the gear stays out of the way and the music gets to breathe.
