Our Story

A Legacy Built on Flavor

Aurum Dining was born from a simple belief: that great food has the power to bring people together. Nestled in the heart of Dubai, our restaurant draws inspiration from Mediterranean and Middle Eastern culinary traditions, reimagined with modern technique and seasonal ingredients.

Our founder, Chef Alessandro Moran, spent two decades refining his craft in kitchens across Italy, Lebanon, and France before opening Aurum in 2018. Every detail, from the hand-selected ceramics to the curated wine list, is designed to create an unforgettable experience.

— Chef Alessandro Moran
