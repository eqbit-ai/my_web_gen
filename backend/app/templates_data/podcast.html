About the Show

Stories That Move the Needle

The Signal is a weekly podcast that dives deep into the minds of the world's most fascinating people. From tech founders and scientists to artists and philosophers, we explore the ideas shaping tomorrow.

Every episode is crafted to leave you with actionable insights and a fresh perspective on what matters most.

  • In-depth interviews averaging 60 minutes
  • Rigorously researched show notes
  • World-class guests from 40+ countries
  • Trusted by Fortune 500 leaders
Your Host

Alex Rivera

Journalist, Author & Podcast Host

Alex Rivera is an award-winning journalist and bestselling author with over 15 years of experience covering technology, culture, and innovation. After a decade at leading publications, Alex launched The Signal to have the kind of conversations that mainstream media rarely allows — long-form, nuanced, and deeply human.

When not behind the microphone, Alex speaks at global conferences, mentors emerging journalists, and works on the next book exploring the intersection of technology and human connection.
