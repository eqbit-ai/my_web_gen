About Me

Designing at the intersection of art & technology

With over 8 years of experience in design and development, I specialize in creating visually stunning and technically robust digital products. From brand identities to full-stack web applications, every project is an opportunity to push creative boundaries.

I believe great design tells a story. My approach combines meticulous attention to detail with bold creative choices, ensuring every pixel serves a purpose and every interaction feels intentional.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring generative art, experimenting with new creative tools, or contributing to open-source design systems.
