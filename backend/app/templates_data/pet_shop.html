Who We Are

PawHaven started in 2018 with a simple mission: to make pet care accessible, joyful, and high-quality. We are a team of passionate pet lovers dedicated to providing the best products, services, and adoption support for your furry, feathery, or scaly family members.

From handpicked premium products to professional grooming and community adoption events, every part of PawHaven is built around the love we share for animals.
