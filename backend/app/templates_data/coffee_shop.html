Our Story

What began as a small passion project in a corner of downtown has grown into a beloved gathering place for coffee lovers. We believe that great coffee is more than a beverage — it's an experience that connects people, sparks conversation, and brightens every day.

Every bean we source is hand-selected from sustainable farms around the world. We roast in small batches right here in our shop, ensuring every cup delivers the freshest, most vibrant flavors possible. From the first sip to the last, you'll taste the difference that genuine care makes.

Our Brewing Methods

Different methods, different stories. Each technique brings out unique characteristics in every bean.

Pour Over

A meditative, hands-on approach that gives us complete control over every variable. The result is a clean, bright cup that highlights the unique terroir of each origin.

Brew Time: 3-4 Minutes

French Press

Full immersion brewing for a rich, full-bodied cup. The metal mesh filter allows the natural oils to pass through, creating a lush, velvety texture.

Brew Time: 4-5 Minutes

Cold Brew

Time does the work. Coarsely ground beans steeped in cold water for 18 hours yield an incredibly smooth, low-acid concentrate that's perfect over ice.

Brew Time: 18 Hours
