PRACTICE AREAS

Four disciplines, one hand

From private villas to cultural institutions — every project, every drawing, signed off by a principal architect.

Residential

Private houses and apartment retrofits where the brief is 'we want to live here for thirty years'. Full architecture and interiors.

Cultural

Museums, libraries, and community centres. Public buildings that earn their place on a street over time.

Commercial

Boutique hotels, offices, and mixed-use buildings for clients who value craft over capital efficiency.

Masterplanning

Urban strategy and campus design for developers and institutions thinking fifteen years ahead, not five.

THE STUDIO

A practice of sixteen. No leverage model.

We're a small studio by design. Every project is led by a principal, drawn by a senior architect, and built under our site supervision. We don't grow faster than we can keep standards.

  • 16 architects, 4 principals — every project led by a partner
  • Two RIBA International Prize shortlists (2021, 2023)
  • Member of the UIA and the Gulf Architects Council
  • Studios in Dubai and Ahmedabad
