COURSES

Four tracks. One standard.

Every cohort is capped at 24 learners and led by a senior practitioner. No pre-recorded lectures — just live teaching and real projects.

Software Engineering

A 16-week cohort covering modern web, cloud, and systems design. Taught by engineers from Meta, Stripe, and Shopify.

Product Design

A 14-week design cohort — research, interaction, prototyping, and a full case-study portfolio. Taught by practicing design leads.

Data & Analytics

Twelve weeks of SQL, Python, statistics, and dashboards. Capstone is a real dataset from a partner company, delivered to their team.

AI Engineering

A new 12-week track on building LLM products — prompting, RAG, evaluations, and deploying real systems. Starts every eight weeks.
