Biography

About the Artist

Nova Eclipse burst onto the music scene in 2019 with a sound that defies easy categorization. Blending pulsing electronic production with soulful vocals and cinematic arrangements, they carved out a niche that resonated with millions worldwide.

Born and raised in London, Nova drew inspiration from the city's underground club culture, late-night radio, and the neon-lit energy of urban nightlife. Their debut EP "First Light" went viral, leading to collaborations with some of the biggest names in music.

Four albums, three world tours, and multiple awards later, Nova Eclipse continues to push boundaries. "Midnight Voltage" represents their most ambitious work yet -- a sonic journey through love, loss, and the electric haze of modern existence.
