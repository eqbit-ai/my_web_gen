OUR PHILOSOPHY

Beauty without the rush

Saskia opened the atelier in 2018 because she couldn't find a salon that didn't feel like a conveyor belt. Every appointment here is private, every product is hand-picked, and your specialist sees one client at a time — the way it used to be done.

  • Six private treatment rooms — no shared spaces
  • Senior specialists only — average 8 years experience
  • Davines, Olaplex, Biologique Recherche, Augustinus Bader
  • Walk-in coffee bar with herbal blends and matcha
